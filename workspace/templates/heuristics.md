Problem-solving and strategic-thinking moves the agent can apply when you're
planning or stuck. A starter set, add the mental moves that reliably help you.

- **Solution-space decomposition** — don't hunt for "the answer"; map the space.
  Break it by constraints: best plan if the middle step goes through A? through B?
- **Assumption factoring** — factor a goal into the values underneath it. Are you
  assuming one implementation when the underlying value could be met another way?
- **Option enumeration** — list all options before evaluating any. Enumerate
  first, judge second.
- **Consult external models** — your own thinking has blind spots; ask people or
  AI to expand the option space, not for consensus.
- **Why not both?** — on either/or orderings, check the real cost of doing them in
  parallel before assuming they're sequential.
- **Physical quick win** — stuck or low on state? do a 2-5 minute physical task to
  bank a small win and reset before the hard thing.
