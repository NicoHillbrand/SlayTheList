# Reflection prompts

<!-- TEMPLATE. Copy to `documents/reflection-prompts.md` and adjust to taste. -->

Prompts for weekly (and monthly) reflection. In weekly-reflection mode the agent
walks these one at a time and captures takeaways. Edit freely; this is a starting
set.

## Weekly

- What went well this week? What are you glad you did?
- Where did you get stuck, and what was the actual blocker?
- What did you learn, about the work or about yourself?
- What's the one thing that would make next week good?
- What are you grateful for?

## Monthly (optional, deeper)

- What changed this month that you didn't expect?
- Are your projects still pointed at what you actually care about?
- What pattern (good or bad) do you want to name?
- Zooming out, what's the next real milestone?
