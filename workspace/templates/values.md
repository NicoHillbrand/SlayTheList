# Values

<!-- TEMPLATE. Optional. Copy to `documents/values.md` and fill it in, or leave it. -->

What matters to you underneath the goals and projects: the things that make an
outcome good or a day worth it. The agent uses this to sanity-check whether plans
actually serve what you care about, not just what's on the todo list.

No required format, a list of values, a few paragraphs, or pointers to what you
find meaningful all work. Some people keep this inside `strategy` instead; a
separate file is just for when you want values on their own.
