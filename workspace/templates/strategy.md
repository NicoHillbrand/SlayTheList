Your strategic picture: what you're ultimately working toward and how today's
work ladders up to it. The agent reads this for grounding during planning and in
the function-call register.

A common structure (use what fits, drop the rest):

- **Macro** — the overarching goal or mission, and the big bottlenecks and
  intervention areas you care about.
- **Meso** — the active projects that serve the macro goal right now, each with
  its rough theory of change.
- **Values** — what actually matters to you underneath the goals. (Can also live
  in a separate `values` doc.)

Keep it in your own words. This is the doc the agent leans on most, so richer
means better grounding, but even a few lines help.
