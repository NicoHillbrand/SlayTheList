# Backlog

A long, unstructured catch-all for ideas, experiments, and someday-maybes: the
stuff that isn't urgent enough to be a todo but you don't want to lose. It also
absorbs stray inbox-type notes.

It's meant to go stale. Every so often the agent can surface one item that seems
ripe given what you're currently working on, as a candidate to promote into your
plans (no pressure to take it). Just add lines freely:

- example: try time-boxing deep work into 90-minute blocks
