# Documents — your live copies

The agent's grounding documents live here: your values, strategy, people
notes, and whatever else you've enabled. Everything in this folder is private
(gitignored) — it never leaves your machine.

Files here start as copies of skeletons from `templates/`, made by the agent
when you decide to enable one, then filled out together. Upstream updates only
ever touch `templates/`, never your copies.
